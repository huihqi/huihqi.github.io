About

I am a postdoctoral researcher interested in understanding the organization and evolution of neural circuits using high-throughput single-neuron projection mapping and spatial molecular approaches.

Research

My research focuses on how neural circuit architectures are organized, diversified, and conserved across species, with a particular interest in linking circuit structure to complex behavior and evolutionary innovation.
